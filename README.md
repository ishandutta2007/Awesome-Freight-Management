**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms



- **[FreightFox](https://www.freightfox.ai/)**  

  AI-powered freight procurement and visibility platform built for Indian manufacturers. Features automated RFQs across **1,000+ vetted transporters**, lane benchmarking against real market rates, contract and spot auctions with audit trails, and modular adoption (Procure, Track, Execute, Settle, Analyze, Decarbonize) . Atul Ltd. achieved **96% shipment visibility**, **3–5% freight cost optimization**, and **300–400+ transporters** onboarded . FMCG enterprise achieved **70% faster dispatch planning** and **8–15% reduced freight leakages** .



- **[Freightify](https://freightify.com/)**  

  AI-first Rate Management System (RMS) for freight forwarders. Unifies rate procurement, pricing, and operations around a single rate intelligence layer. Supports **30+ LCL/FCL carriers** and **90+ airlines** with live rates. Features instant rate search, automated margin rules, ancillary charge inclusion (Demurrage & Detention, Surcharges, Arbitrary Charges), multi-modal coverage (Ocean, Air, Road, Rail), and mobile rate management. Customers report **50% reduced operational cost**, **92% reduced processing time**, and **30% increased quote win ratio** . Deep integration with CargoWise ensures operations teams work with accurate commercial data .



- **[FreightPOP](https://www.freightpop.com/)**  

  AI supply chain platform unifying order management, warehouse management, and transportation management. **1,500+ integrations** across ERP (NetSuite, SAP, Microsoft Dynamics, Epicor, Syspro, Acumatica), carriers, and marketplaces. Supports **parcel, LTL, FTL, ocean, rail, and international air**. Features rate shopping, auto-dispatch, invoice auditing (**8–15% savings**), and FreightPOP AI with Copilot and agentic automation (Tracking Agent, Accessorial Recommendation Agent) . **99.99% platform uptime**.



- **[Logistaas](https://logistaas.com/)**  

  TMS purpose-built for freight forwarders, trusted in **75+ countries**. Features shipment management, sales & pricing, finance & accounting, customer portal, and e-invoicing support with localization in **30+ countries**. Integrates with rate platforms like CargoFive and external accounting systems .



- **[CargoWise](https://www.wisetechglobal.com/)**  

  Logistics execution platform with integrated customs compliance, classification, and duty calculation. Used by freight forwarders and customs brokers globally.



- **[Magaya](https://www.magaya.com/)**  

  Logistics and supply chain software for freight forwarders, NVOCCs, and 3PLs. Features warehouse management, cargo tracking, and accounting.



- **[Descartes](https://www.descartes.com/)**  

  Global logistics and trade compliance platform with customs declaration filing, HS classification, and tariff calculation.



- **[Kuebix](https://www.kuebix.com/)**  

  Transportation management system (acquired by FreightWise) with community load matching and rate benchmarking.



- **[Transplace](https://www.transplace.com/)**  

  Managed transportation and logistics platform (Uber Freight). Features TMS, brokerage, and supply chain consulting.



- **[MercuryGate](https://www.mercurygate.com/)**  

  Transportation management system for shippers, brokers, and carriers. Features multi-modal planning, execution, and settlement.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects



- **[Fleetbase](https://github.com/fleetbase/fleetbase)**  

  **Modular logistics and supply chain operating system (LSOS)** — the most comprehensive open-source logistics platform available (AGPL-3.0). **4,231 GitHub stars** . Features fleet & transport management (FleetOps extension), headless commerce & marketplace (Storefront extension), order management, and a rich extension ecosystem. Ember.js frontend with PHP API. Deployment via Docker. **The most feature-complete open-source alternative to CargoWise or Descartes.**



- **[Trenova](https://github.com/emoss08/trenova)**  

  **AI-driven asset-based Transportation Management System** (open-source, 85 GitHub stars) . Written in Go with PostgreSQL, GraphQL, and React. Covers full order-to-cash cycle: shipment entry, dispatch, driver assignment, rating and accessorials (formula-based rate calculation, fuel surcharge, detention rules), billing and invoicing, accounting, and compliance. EDI 204/210/214/990/997 partner workflows, telematics (Samsara), PC*MILER, AS2. **The most modern open-source TMS for asset-based carriers.**



- **[tms (loadpartner)](https://github.com/VidarSten/tms)**  

  **Open-source TMS for freight brokers** (66 GitHub stars) . Built with Laravel, Inertia.js, and React. Production instance at tms.loadpartner.io. Features freight brokerage workflows with modern PHP/JS stack. **The most focused open-source TMS specifically for freight brokers.**



- **[freight_management (Solufy)](https://github.com/Solufy-ERPNext-Apps/freight_management)**  

  Open-source ERPNext module managing all freight operations (Air, Ocean, Land). Supports **FCL and LCL** for ocean, **FTL and LTL** for land . Comprehensive freight forwarding operations platform built on ERPNext ecosystem.



- **[LogiTrack](https://github.com/automaster5013/LogiTrack)**  

  **Event-driven logistics control tower** for real-time fleet, warehouse, and delivery operations (open-source) . Simulates order, warehouse, and fleet operations without dedicated GPS hardware. Features transactional outbox, Kafka, idempotent consumers, retry/DLQ recovery, MapLibre map with GPS trajectories, SSE-based vehicle/alert updates, OpenTelemetry/Tempo/Prometheus/Grafana observability, and GitHub Actions/AWS OIDC deployment. **The most architecturally modern open-source logistics control tower.**



- **[Ghana Cargo & Logistics](https://github.com/edzeame-del/ghana-cargo-logistics)**  

  Comprehensive cargo and logistics management system for the Ghanaian market (open-source) . Features vessel management with MarineTraffic integration, cargo tracking with **28,000+ records**, dual Google Sheets sync, search analytics with heat maps, automatic ETA calculation, and bilingual (Chinese/English) support. React + TypeScript frontend, Node.js/Express backend, PostgreSQL with Drizzle ORM. **The most regionally-adapted open-source freight platform.**

## Disclaimer



- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.

- Freight management systems handling shipment and carrier data must comply with applicable transportation regulations, customs requirements, and data protection laws.

- **Open-source freight management is maturing but fragmented by role.** Fleetbase provides a modular logistics OS . Trenova targets asset-based carriers . tms (loadpartner) targets freight brokers . **No single open-source platform provides the end-to-end freight forwarding capabilities of CargoWise or Freightify** . Integration work is required.

- **Freight procurement automation is a commercial strength.** FreightFox's AI-powered RFQs across 1,000+ transporters and lane benchmarking , and Freightify's rate management with 30+ LCL/FCL carriers and 90+ airlines , represent capabilities that open-source alternatives do not yet match.

- **Modular adoption is the pragmatic path.** FreightFox emphasizes modular adoption (Procure, Track, Execute, Settle, Analyze) , and Fleetbase provides extensions . Choose components that solve immediate pain points rather than attempting full-stack replacement.

- Self-hosted open-source solutions require proper security hardening, EDI/carrier integration, and ongoing maintenance. Freight data is operationally sensitive — access controls and audit trails are essential.
